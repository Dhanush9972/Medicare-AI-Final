CRITICAL WARNING

This information is for EMERGENCY REFERENCE ONLY. Always call emergency services (911) for life-threatening situations. Consult healthcare professionals before administering any medication.

Emergency System Limitations

  • This system provides informational guidance only during emergencies
  • Always prioritize calling emergency services (911) for life-threatening situations
  • Do not delay professional medical treatment based on these recommendations
  • Medication administration should only be done by trained medical personnel
  • Individual medical conditions and allergies may contraindicate these medications
